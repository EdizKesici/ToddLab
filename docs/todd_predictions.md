# Todd's predictions, checked against the books

A track record of Emmanuel Todd's predictions, one row per prediction, in which every **citation has been checked against the exact text of the scanned books**.

## What this document is

Each row is one atomized, individually verifiable prediction: a direct claim with a named target and/or a dated horizon (hedged hypotheses, conditional chains and general laws are left out as unfalsifiable). It is scored against the record up to 2026.

## How to read the table

- **#**: running number (1 to 80). Rows are grouped by book, then follow the order of the chapters.
- **Book**: title and year of the first edition, in the form used by `todd_core.csv`.
- **Chapter**: name of the chapter or part of the book where the quote stands (`Préface`, `Introduction`, `Conclusion`, `Annexe II`, `Troisième partie`...), without number, section or page.
- **Prediction**: the atomized claim, in English.
- **Outcome**: what happened, assessed from a 2026 vantage point.
- **Verdict**: see the legend below.
- **Citation**: Todd's own words in French, **as printed in the scanned edition**, with the spelling of the edition (e.g. *Etats-Unis*); scanning (OCR) errors have been corrected. A citation may run over several sentences when the prediction needs the context. `[...]` marks a passage left out of the quotation and `[text]` an editorial insertion.
- **Citation\_translation**: an English rendering of the whole citation.

**Outcome and Verdict are DRAFT assessments** written in 2026 and meant to be reviewed and corrected by the reader. The Citation is the verified part.

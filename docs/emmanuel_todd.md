# Who is Emmanuel Todd?

ToddLab borrows its founding method from Todd's 1976 essay; this file documents who he is, how he works, why it worked, and what he got right (and not).


## 1. Emmanuel Todd

Born 3 May 1951, Saint-Germain-en-Laye, France. Historian, demographer, anthropologist.
     Studied at Sciences Po, then took a history doctorate at Cambridge, working with the Cambridge Group for the History of Population and Social Structure.
     Professional demographer: researcher at INED (French national demographic institute) from 1975, later research director (CNRS).
     Author of 20+ books in two registers: demographic anthropology (family systems of the world) and applied forecasting essays.


## 2. The method

### 2.1 Hard indicators: "bodies don't lie"

Todd's epistemology: births, deaths, literacy results and anthropometric data are the hardest facts societies produce. They are recorded for administrative and legal reasons, regardless of the regime's ideology, unlike declared data (GDP, plans, opinions).

### 2.2 Family systems as the deep layer

Todd maps the world's family structures (absolute nuclear, egalitarian nuclear, stem, exogamous community, anomic...) and claims that ideologies are secularized family systems, such as communism taking root in exogamous-community zones (Slavic world, China), English liberalism in the absolute-nuclear zone. Full taxonomy in "L'Origine des systèmes familiaux" (2011) — "The Origin of Family Systems".

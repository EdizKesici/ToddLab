# Who is Emmanuel Todd?

ToddLab borrows its founding method from Todd's 1976 essay *La Chute finale*. This file documents who he is, how he works, the founding case, and where to find what he predicted and how it turned out.


## 1. Emmanuel Todd

Born 3 May 1951, Saint-Germain-en-Laye, France. Historian, demographer, anthropologist.

- Studied at Sciences Po, then took a history doctorate at Cambridge, working with the Cambridge Group for the History of Population and Social Structure.
- Professional demographer: researcher at INED (French national demographic institute) from 1975, later research director (CNRS).
- Author of 20+ books in two registers: demographic anthropology (family systems of the world) and applied forecasting essays.


## 2. The method

### 2.1 Hard indicators: "bodies don't lie"

Todd's epistemology: births, deaths, literacy results and anthropometric data are the hardest facts societies produce. They are recorded for administrative and legal reasons, regardless of the regime's ideology, unlike declared data (GDP, plans, opinions).

### 2.2 Family systems as the deep layer

Todd maps the world's family structures (absolute nuclear, egalitarian nuclear, stem, exogamous community, anomic...) and claims that ideologies are secularized family systems, such as communism taking root in exogamous-community zones (Slavic world, China), English liberalism in the absolute-nuclear zone. Full taxonomy in "L'Origine des systèmes familiaux" (2011), "The Origin of Family Systems".

### 2.3 The founding case

In *La Chute finale* (1976), Todd read the rise of Soviet infant mortality in the 1970s as a hard signal of a system in decomposition, and announced the erosion or collapse of the USSR within ten, twenty or thirty years. The USSR was dissolved in December 1991. The case, and why the same indicator tells different stories depending on the source, is worked out in [`the-measurement-problem.md`](the-measurement-problem.md).

### 2.4 From the books to the indicators

The metrics Todd cites across his books are listed in `todd_core.csv` (one row per metric and book, with a citation count). They are the indicators flagged `todd_core` in ToddLab and the basis of the "Todd" weighting of the score. The corpus keeps only the books in which Todd makes predictions.


## 3. His predictions

Todd's books contain explicit predictions, with a named target or a dated horizon. [`todd_predictions.md`](todd_predictions.md) lists them in one table, grouped by book. Each row gives the prediction, Todd's own words (the quotation, checked against the scanned books), an English translation, what happened since, and a verdict: verified, partially verified or not realized. That table is where to look to see what he got right and what he got wrong.

The quotations are the verified part. The outcomes and verdicts are drafts, written in 2026 and meant to be reviewed. Books in which Todd makes no real predictions are not listed. The website shows a short version of the list at `/predictions`: the predictions of each book and a verdict symbol.
